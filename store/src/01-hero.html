Macro Deck 3 plugin

IP Camera

Live camera tiles on your deck. Snapshot, MJPEG, RTSP and ONVIF, with the Xiaomi Mi 360 handled through go2rtc.

RTSP via ffmpeg MJPEG and snapshots ONVIF discovery

The plugin reads the camera on the Macro Deck computer and relays the picture to the deck. No cloud account is stored in the plugin.
